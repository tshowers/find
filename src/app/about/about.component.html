About FindA Taliferro Tech product · Part of TODD

Find is Search that starts with the right result.

Find is a search and results application for the web and iPhone. It puts the strongest result first, then gives you enough context to decide whether you need to keep going.

What it is

One destination for useful results.

Find turns natural-language questions into a focused results experience. Instead of making you choose between ten blue links, it leads with the result most likely to help.

Category

Search and results application.

Find belongs to the search and discovery category. It combines web results with structured experiences for news, weather, sports, restaurants, conversions, movies, and questions.

Who made it

Taliferro Tech, LLC.

Find is made by Taliferro Tech and is part of TODD, the Momentum System. Taliferro Tech creates connected software products that help people move from information to action.

The product idea

Most search tools optimize for clicks. Find optimizes for clarity.

Find gives you the strongest result first, keeps the source close, and lets you refine without losing the question that started the search.

When a query needs a different shape—forecast, conversion, ratings, nearby places, or a grounded summary of results—Find changes the experience around the task.
