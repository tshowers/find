About Find

Find for iPhone Download Find from the App Store ↗
01

One destination, not a list

Find picks the single strongest result instead of showing you 10 blue links. Swipe to see alternatives.

02

Context pills & drill-down

Tap a pill in the bottom bar to refine your search context. Find keeps your original query intact and swaps the active refinement instead of stacking duplicates.

03

People → Lead Vault

Find shows the best web results first. When a query looks like a person, buyer, or prospect, you can continue the search in Lead Vault when you’re ready.

04

The philosophy

Most search tools optimize for clicks. Find optimizes for the right result. We believe the best search experience ends with you knowing something — not choosing between 10 links that might.
