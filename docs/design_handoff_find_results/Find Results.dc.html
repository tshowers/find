Heat pump systems
A heat pump moves heat instead of making it. In winter it pulls heat from the outside air or ground and brings it indoors; in summer it reverses, carrying heat out of the house like an air conditioner. Because it transfers heat rather than burning fuel, it can deliver up to three times more energy than it uses.

Action rules
Find shows only the actions it has data for. The first action is the main one for the result type (Visit site, Directions, Email, IMDb page, Open recipe). Call uses tel: and Email uses mailto:, so iPhone and Mac open Phone or FaceTime and Mail. Directions links to maps.apple.com on Apple devices and Google Maps everywhere else. Add to calendar downloads an .ics file, which opens Calendar. Copy actions put the link, address, email or ingredient list on the clipboard.
Backend gaps: FindRankedResult already has address, phone and hours. Email, a person type with role and company, and a recipe type with time, yield and ingredients would need new fields.
Find: result page, redesigned
Each result gets an action bar built from what Find pulls out of the page: an address becomes Directions, a phone number becomes Call, an email becomes Email. On Mac and iPhone these open Apple Maps, the dialer or FaceTime, and Mail. On desktop the 19 alternates sit in a side rail you can see.

How the action bar is built
Find shows only the actions it has data for, in this order: the main action for the result type (Visit site, Directions, Trailer), then Call, Email, Directions or Website, Copy, Share. Links use tel:, mailto: and maps.apple.com, so Mac and iPhone open Phone or FaceTime, Mail and Apple Maps directly, and other devices get the web or Google Maps version. Share uses the system share sheet where the browser supports it.
