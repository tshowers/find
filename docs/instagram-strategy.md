# Instagram Strategy: Find by 16Ahead

Account: @findby16ahead (Professional)

## Posting schedule

Posting on a steady schedule matters more than posting a lot. Two posts every week for months beats five a week for two weeks and then nothing.

| Day | Format | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tuesday | How-to Reel (1080×1920) | Reach people who don't follow yet. One search per video: a team, a restaurant, weather, a shop. |
| Friday | "The weekly Find" carousel (1080×1350) | Saves and shares. Turns new viewers into followers. |
| Most days | One Story | Keeps Find at the front of followers' Story bar. One find, a screenshot, a link sticker. |

Post in the evening, about 6–8pm.

After one month, check Insights. If Reels bring in most new followers, add a second weekly Reel before adding more carousels.

## Weekly Find carousel

- Cover slide, one slide per shop or site (4–5), closing slide asking people to try Find.
- Portrait 4:5. When uploading, click the crop icon and pick **4:5**, or the bottom of each slide gets cut off.
- One or two factual lines per shop, written in your own words and different every week.
- Leave out sales or dates that will expire before the post goes up.
- Tag each shop on its slide. Optionally invite one as a Collab, which only works from the phone app.

## How-to Reels

- Show the result in the first 1–2 seconds.
- On-screen captions, since most people watch with the sound off.
- Use Instagram's audio library or your own voice. No copyrighted music.
- Leave out photos and logos you don't own, such as team or league images. Use plain color blocks instead.
- Make sure stats and dates are still current on the day you post.

## Avoiding spam flags

Instagram doesn't flag a post because AI wrote it. It flags the patterns AI-generated posts tend to share.

- **Captions:** Write each one yourself. Don't reuse wording, emoji patterns or calls to action ("Link in bio! DM me!").
- **Hashtags:** 3–5 relevant ones, changed from post to post.
- **Pacing:** No bursts of several posts in an hour.
- **Engagement bait:** Skip "comment YES," "tag 3 friends" and "follow for part 2."
- **Links:** Put them in the bio or a Story link sticker, never in captions, and don't use shortened links.
- **Third-party tools:** No scheduling or automation tools that log in with your password. Use Meta's own tools.
- **Video:** No video carrying another platform's watermark.
- **Activity:** No mass liking, following or commenting.
- **Status:** Check Settings → Account Status now and then. It shows whether your content is being kept out of recommendations, and you can appeal from there.

## New account warm-up (first week)

- Complete the profile first: photo, bio, link (link added from the phone app: Edit profile → Links).
- Follow and like 5–10 local businesses a day, the way a person would.
- Post 1–2 Stories, then the first carousel around day 5–7.
- Create the next product's account a few weeks later, not on the same day.
